## Acceptance design and limits

- Standard homeowner/contractor signup, contractor-invitation homeowner signup and local-customer-claim signup retain their existing Auth call and profile/referral logic. Sign-in and existing-user claim/connect flows do not gain invented assent.
- Shared `LegalConsent` displays exact archived assent wording, required documents, version and separate meaning of privacy acknowledgment. Contractor signup includes the contractor agreement. Links open in a separate tab to preserve form state. The required checkbox never writes marketing consent.
- Client preflight compares server bundle ID and SHA-256 against the actual bundled text. Database trigger independently rejects missing/stale/malformed assent or unexpected fields. Actor and time come from `NEW.id` and database clock; no client time/email/IP/claim token is copied to the ledger.
- A submitted context label is untrusted descriptive evidence; it does not prove invitation validity or confer permissions. Existing invitation/claim RPCs still enforce access. U.S./adult statement is an attestation, not geolocation or age verification.
- Capture runs in the Auth insert transaction. A rejection rolls back account/profile/referral writes; success records signup submission even when email confirmation is still pending. It is not proof of identity, email ownership, a signature certificate or subsequent reacceptance.
- Users can read only their own ledger rows. No browser or service-role insert/update/delete grants. UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE triggers protect the archive/ledger even from accidental privileged DML. Privileged administrators can still change database objects; this is not cryptographically immutable storage against the database owner.
- No FK from ledger to Auth/profile: deleting an account cannot silently cascade away legal evidence. UUID is personal data; eventual lawful disposal remains separately controlled and approval-gated. No existing account backfill occurs.
- `servsync-versioned-legal-acceptance.sql` installs inert schema/functions only. `servsync-legal-policy-archive.sql` archives exact bytes only. `scripts/legal/activate-policy.sql` checks completeness/effective date, installs capture and moves the current pointer in one transaction. Preflight refuses a pointer without the active trigger.
- Preserve published JSON/SQL bytes permanently under their version identifiers. During this unissued candidate review they may be revised and regenerated; after first activation, any change uses a new ID/file/archive entry. Never rewrite old acceptance rows. Policy versions share one bundle ID because all corresponding documents/assent are archived together with one hash.

6. Rollback: prefer a forward repair preserving the archive/ledger and mandatory acceptance. Restoring frontend requires a compatible approved bundle/pointer; stale clients will fail closed. Never delete rows, backfill consent or disable capture to hide a failure. Any pointer, trigger or production rollback requires explicit approval.
