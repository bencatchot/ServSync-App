# Current Beta Limitations

Status: internal product-truth draft for human validation and later onboarding/support reconciliation. Recheck runtime state before external use.

## What the limited beta is evaluating

ServSync currently supports a coherent core loop for Customers and properties, Draft-first work preparation, Estimates and customer responses, Jobs, Invoices, supported offline payment recording, PDFs, Price Book authoring/import, role-aware access, homeowner views, and durable home/service history where current workflow records support it.

The study evaluates whether the intended small contractor and homeowner cohorts can understand and use that current loop. It does not imply broad field-service-suite parity.

## Boundaries to state candidly

| Area | Current beta boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Payments | Production online card/ACH collection and live Stripe payment movement are not active. The study uses the supported offline payment-recording path only. Do not describe ServSync as collecting online payments. |
| Scheduling and dispatch | Current schedule, appointments, assignment, and Job states support a light workflow. ServSync is not a mature multi-crew dispatch, routing, or optimization system. |
| Reporting | Current dashboards, history, statuses, and attention views are not a deep business-intelligence or built-in reporting suite. |
| Integrations | Broad accounting, CRM, calendar, marketing, catalog, and app-marketplace integrations are not live. Do not promise QuickBooks or provider sync. |
| Mobile | ServSync has responsive mobile web workflows. Native iOS/Android apps, offline field operation, and broad device-scale market proof are not established. |
| Migration/onboarding | CSV/XLSX Price Book tools and safe reconciliation exist, but broad white-glove migration, every provider format, and scaled onboarding/support are not proven. |
| Dispatch/automation | Advanced dispatch, route planning, automated outreach, and broad workflow automation are outside current beta capability. |
| Market proof | ServSync has extensive internal functional/security/recovery evidence but limited independent user and production-tenure evidence. Do not claim adoption, ratings, time savings, or competitive superiority. |
| Home History | Home History represents supported ServSync workflow records for a home. It does not automatically import every outside service interaction or all prior guest/link activity. |
| Connected versus account-free Customers | Contractors can support connected and not-connected Customer paths. A homeowner account/connection is optional and must not be described as automatically absorbing all earlier guest activity. |

## Initial cohort fit to test

The most credible early cohort is an owner/operator or very small office/field team that values organized Customer, Estimate, Job, Invoice, and homeowner-history continuity and can operate during beta with offline payment recording and light scheduling.

Do not recruit participants by attacking Jobber, Housecall Pro, or another competitor. Present the current boundary plainly and let behavior and later neutral comparison establish fit.

## Not a promise

This document is not public copy, a service-level agreement, a launch approval, or proof that every listed capability is suitable for every trade. Update it when runtime capability changes, and resolve conflicts in favor of verified current product behavior.
