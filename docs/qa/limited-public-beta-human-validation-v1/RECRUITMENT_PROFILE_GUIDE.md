# Recruitment and Profile Guide

## Contractor cohort

Recruit 5-8 small service contractor owner/operators or very small-team operators who directly participate in Customer, Estimate, Job, and Invoice work.

Target mix:

- varied home-service trades; do not require HVAC;
- primarily owner/operator or small office/field teams;
- ordinary phone/web comfort, not only software enthusiasts;
- no prior ServSync training;
- approximately 2-3 with current or prior Jobber, Housecall Pro, or comparable FSM experience.

Do not over-recruit one trade or only people already favorable to the product idea.

Suggested screener:

1. What type of service work does your business perform?
2. About how many people work in the business?
3. Which parts of Customer, estimating, field work, and invoicing do you personally handle?
4. What device do you normally use for work away from a desk?
5. Have you used field-service software? Which product, for approximately how long, and is it current or prior use?
6. Have you previously used or been trained on ServSync?

Exclude or classify separately:

- ServSync designers, developers, regular testers, or trained users;
- people who do not perform or understand the target contractor workflow;
- participants unable to consent;
- vendor consultants whose expertise would dominate ordinary first-use behavior.

## Homeowner cohort

Recruit 5-8 homeowners who have hired home-service contractors, use ordinary apps/websites, have no prior ServSync training, and vary in technical comfort.

Suggested screener:

1. Have you hired a home-service contractor in the last few years?
2. Which device do you normally use to review appointments, quotes, or bills?
3. How comfortable are you completing ordinary online forms or reviewing documents?
4. Have you previously used or been trained on ServSync?

Avoid relying only on friends/family who already understand the product concept when more neutral participants are available.

## Study profile data

Retain only study-relevant ranges or categories:

- anonymous ID;
- cohort and trade category;
- owner/operator versus office role;
- business-size range;
- years-in-business range;
- FSM familiarity and product category;
- primary device;
- self-described technical comfort;
- general home-service hiring experience.

Keep recruitment names, contact details, scheduling, and compensation records outside Git in approved owner-controlled storage. The repository should contain no participant list.

## Competitor-experienced follow-up

Run the same neutral tasks first. Only after task completion ask:

- What felt familiar?
- What felt unfamiliar?
- What took more effort than expected?
- What took less effort?
- Was anything important missing?
- Was anything present that you do not normally get from your current software?
- Which system would you prefer for this particular workflow, and why?
- What would stop you from changing systems?
- What would make switching worth the effort?

Do not tell participants during tasks that the study is a competitive comparison. One participant's experience is not a market conclusion.
