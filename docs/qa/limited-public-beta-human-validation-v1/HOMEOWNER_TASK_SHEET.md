# Homeowner Participant Task Sheet

Participant ID: `H__`

Session ID: `H__-YYYYMMDD-A`

Use only the fictional information supplied for this session. Do not enter a real address, contractor, payment, photo, or document.

## Scenario

You own the fictional home provided by the facilitator. A home-service issue needs attention, and the contractor uses ServSync. You will request help, respond to the contractor, review the work and cost, and later find the record.

## Tasks

1. **Get oriented.** Take a look around and tell the facilitator what you think this app or site is for.
2. **Ask for service.** The problem on the supplied home-service card needs attention. Use ServSync to ask for service.
3. **Respond to the contractor.** The contractor has proposed times. Review what they sent and continue appropriately.
4. **Review the work proposal.** The contractor sent something related to the work. Review it and decide what you want to do.
5. **Check progress.** Find out what is happening with the work and whether the contractor has sent any updates.
6. **Review the charge.** The contractor says the work is complete. Find what you were charged and whether a balance remains.
7. **Find the old record.** Six months later, find who performed the work, what was done, when it happened, and what it cost.
8. **Return for another need.** You now have another issue at the same property. Show the facilitator what you would do.

After each task, the facilitator will ask how confident you are that you completed it correctly and how difficult or easy it felt. There are no trick questions, and you may stop at any time.
