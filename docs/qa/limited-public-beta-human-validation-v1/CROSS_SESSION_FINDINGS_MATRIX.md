## Synthesis checkpoints

After participant 2, 4, and final cohort completion:

- merge only findings supported by the same underlying behavior;
- keep superficially similar but causally different findings separate;
- reconsider severity using the guide, not count alone;
- identify protocol or fixture defects separately;
- note whether later participants confirm, contradict, or contextualize earlier findings;
- do not redesign mid-study unless a P0 or clear P1 makes continued testing unsafe or wasteful.
