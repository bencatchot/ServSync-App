# Homeowner Facilitator Script

Use with the [Master Protocol](HUMAN_VALIDATION_MASTER_PROTOCOL.md), [Homeowner Task Sheet](HOMEOWNER_TASK_SHEET.md), and [Observation Template](SESSION_OBSERVATION_TEMPLATE.md).

## Before the participant arrives

1. Complete runbook preflight and record target, session ID, baseline, fixture IDs, and checkpoint sequence.
2. Confirm the account is an approved test homeowner connected only to fictional records.
3. Prepare the [Fictional Scenario Cards](FICTIONAL_SCENARIO_CARDS.md), proposed appointment state, sent Estimate, active/completed Job state, Invoice state, and historical record.
4. Verify each state against the current app before the session. Do not promise Home History content that the fixture does not contain.
5. Ensure all contact endpoints are non-delivery test values. Never send to a real phone or email.
6. Open at the normal signed-in homeowner landing view. Do not open Requests, Activity, Estimates, Invoices, or Home History for the participant.

## Opening

Read the master opening script and confirm consent. Say:

> I will describe things you want to accomplish. Please work as you normally would and say what you are looking for or expecting.

Do not explain the homeowner value proposition, connected-account model, Home History, or navigation.

## Task 1 - Orientation

Read:

> You have a home-service issue and your contractor uses ServSync. Take a look around and tell me what you think this app or site is for.

Observe home/property context, current-work interpretation, and the participant's own value proposition. Do not correct them during the task.

## Task 2 - Request service

Give the fictional issue card. Read:

> The problem on this card needs attention. Use ServSync to ask for service.

Observe request discovery, contractor relationship, property choice, form burden, status, and expected next step. Record exact test record IDs in the private mutation ledger.

## Task 3 - Appointment response

Move to the prepared proposal state. Read:

> Your contractor has responded. Review what they sent and continue appropriately.

Do not say "accept an appointment." Observe proposed-time comprehension, acceptance/decline choice, status, and expected next contact.

## Task 4 - Estimate

Move to the sent-Estimate checkpoint. Read:

> Your contractor has sent you something related to the work. Review it and decide what you want to do.

Observe whether they identify it as an Estimate, amount and scope clarity, supported accept/decline/change-request actions, consequences, and confidence. Do not coach the desired response.

## Task 5 - Job status and communication

Move to the prepared active-work state. Read:

> You want to know what is happening with the work and whether your contractor has sent any updates. Find that information.

Observe messages, Activity, Job context, system-event versus human-message interpretation, and status clarity.

## Task 6 - Invoice

Move to the prepared Invoice state. Read:

> The contractor says the work is complete and you want to see what you were charged.

Observe Invoice discovery, total and balance, paid/unpaid interpretation, and PDF/document access. Do not imply online payment is available.

## Task 7 - Historical work

Move to the prepared historical state. Read:

> Six months later, you want to remember who performed this work, what was done, when it happened, and what it cost. Find the record.

Do not name Home History. Observe contractor identity, date, amount, service description, Invoice relationship, and whether the participant expects all outside work or guest activity to appear automatically.

## Task 8 - Returning service need

Read:

> You now have another issue at the same property. Show me what you would do.

Observe whether ServSync feels like an ongoing home/contractor relationship or a collection of old documents. Do not require a submission if it would duplicate test data unnecessarily; record the point at which the participant demonstrates their intended path.

## Mobile evidence

At least one homeowner task must run entirely at approximately 390 x 844 or on an actual phone. Prefer service request, Estimate review, or historical work. Record keyboard obstruction, overflow, reachability, scrolling, and mistaken taps.

## After each task

Record outcome and behavior, then ask:

> From 1 to 5, how confident are you that you completed that correctly?

> From 1 to 5, where 1 is very difficult and 5 is very easy, how would you rate that task?

## Post-session questions

- What did you think ServSync was keeping for you?
- What felt most clear?
- What took more effort than expected?
- What, if anything, felt risky or uncertain?
- What would you expect to happen after asking for service again?
- Was anything important missing from this experience?

Do not ask whether Home History is useful. Close, sign out, then complete exact cleanup and verification.
