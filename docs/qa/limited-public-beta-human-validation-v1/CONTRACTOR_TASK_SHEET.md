# Contractor Participant Task Sheet

Participant ID: `C__`

Session ID: `C__-YYYYMMDD-A`

Use only the fictional information supplied for this session. Do not enter real customer, property, or payment information.

## Scenario

You run a small residential service business. A new customer needs work at their home. You will organize the request, prepare the work, carry it through completion and billing, and later find the historical record.

The facilitator will provide the fictional Customer card and service card needed for the tasks.

## Tasks

1. **Get oriented.** Take a look around and tell the facilitator where you would go if you needed to manage the work you have going on.
2. **Add the new Customer.** A new customer called about work at their home. Add them using the supplied Customer card.
3. **Prepare the work.** The Customer needs the work described on the service card. Prepare an estimate for them, including all listed work and quantities.
4. **Find a Customer.** Start new work for the specific Customer named by the facilitator.
5. **Continue after acceptance.** The Customer accepted the estimate. Continue the work from here.
6. **Finish the work.** You are at the property and have completed the work. Update ServSync appropriately using the supplied completion notes.
7. **Bill the Customer.** The work is complete. Bill the Customer.
8. **Record payment.** The Customer paid using the offline method supplied by the facilitator. Record that payment.
9. **Find the history.** Several months later, the Customer asks what work was completed and what they paid. Find that information.
10. **Phone-size task.** On the supplied phone-size view or device, complete the core task named by the facilitator.

After each task, the facilitator will ask how confident you are that you completed it correctly and how difficult or easy it felt. There are no trick questions, and you may stop at any time.
