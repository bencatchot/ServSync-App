# ServSync Flagship Introduction Video v2

## Purpose

This local owner-review video introduces ServSync through one two-sided home-service story. It begins with the homeowner search problem, shows the small-contractor visibility and organization problem, then uses the real dedicated-Demo ServSync interface to show Discover and the connected service workflow.

The recording is Marketing support media. It does not publish, upload a Marketing asset, approve a pairing, or authorize a provider request.

## Product-Truth Boundary

The Discover story is:

`contractor-created content -> homeowner browses -> contractor profile -> homeowner chooses whether to connect`

The homeowner's actual need enters ServSync only after connection through the separate Service Request workflow. The video does not show or imply homeowner-created Discover posts, ranking, reviews, verification, lead scoring, bidding, availability matching, social-media importing, automated lead generation, dispatch, online payment, or accounting integration.

All visible identities and business records are fictional, registry-owned data in the dedicated Demo project. The Discover feed, profile, connection prompt, Service Request, accepted Estimate, completed Job, draft Invoice, Home History row, finalized report, and signup screen are current ServSync product surfaces. The Invoice and finalized report are created through the ordinary product contracts rather than recording-only substitutes.

## Cedar Narration

Use `gpt-4o-mini-tts` with the built-in `cedar` voice and this exact script:

> Need a deck built, an outlet repaired, or a water heater replaced? For many homeowners, the search starts on social media, then becomes a list of names with little service context.
>
> Small contractors see the other side: good businesses competing to be noticed. The idea behind ServSync grew from firsthand experience with that challenge. Finding customers is hard enough. Keeping requests, estimates, jobs, invoices, and records organized should not make the work harder.
>
> ServSync is being built to help both sides. In Discover, homeowners can browse work, practical tips, and services shared by local contractors, open a profile, and decide whether to connect.
>
> Once connected, a service request ties the need to a home. The contractor prepares an estimate. The homeowner approves it. The agreed work moves into a job. Billing stays connected, while finalized reports remain with the home in Home History.
>
> ServSync is in limited public beta. Homeowners and small contractors can create a free account, try the platform, and help make home service easier to keep connected.

Speech direction: knowledgeable small-business owner calmly showing how the platform works; conversational, warm, practical, and understated; moderate natural pace with small pauses; no announcer emphasis or advertising delivery.

The narration begins after a short visual pause. It must end before the final signup hold; the screen recording is never sped up to fit the audio.
